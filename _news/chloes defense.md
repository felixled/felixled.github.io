On October 28, 2026 [Chloe]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}#chloe) successfully defended her PhD! Her thesis is titled "Mathematical structures in quantum information processing: Convexity, symmetry, and operator algebras". Chloe was jointly advised by [Eric Chitambar](https://quantum-entangled.ece.illinois.edu/) and myself. You can browse some of her thesis work (and other papers not included in his thesis) on the [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=Kim%2C+Chloe&searchtype=author&abstracts=show&order=-announced_date_first&size=50). It's been a pleasure supervising you, Chloe, and all the best for the future!
