Lists of references to topics in mathematics and quantum information science

Created on October 28, 2026

2026   ·   references

On this page I am collecting selected references to topics in mathematics and quantum information theory that intersect with my own research interests. I compiled these lists having graduate students in mind who want to start working on questions related to these topics. Hopefully, they are also useful for more experienced researchers.

List of lists

  • Introductions to quantum information
  • Entanglement theory
  • Haar measure and k-designs
  • Quantum channel capacities
  • Representation theory and its applications in quantum information theory

How to navigate these lists

I’ve tried to include both introductory reading material (textbooks, lecture notes, review articles, etc.) and more advanced material (papers). Every topic below starts with a subsection “Introductory reading” that is a good place to start. Except for the first section, which covers introductions to quantum information as a whole.

Please keep in mind that, while aimed at a general audience, these lists still mostly reflect my personal view of these topics and are heavily biased towards my own understanding of the material and personal preferences!

Please get in touch with me if you have any questions on how to start with these topics, or if I have forgotten any resources you think should be included!

Introductions to quantum information

This section lists some introductory texts and reading material to get started in the field of quantum information. Since this field is multidisciplinary by its very nature, spanning Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, and Information Theory, one can approach it from different angles this list is trying to cover. The following legend is used: Math Mathematics; Phys Physics; CS Computer Science; IT Information Theory.
